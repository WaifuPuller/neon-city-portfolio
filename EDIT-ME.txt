===============================================================================
  NEON CITY - HOW TO CHANGE EVERYTHING
  A plain-English guide. No coding knowledge assumed.
===============================================================================

THE ONE FILE YOU EDIT
---------------------

    src/config/portfolio.ts

That is it. Every word, link, project, skill and job on the site comes from
that one file. You never need to open anything else.

Open it in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, whatever you like).


HOW TO SEE YOUR CHANGES
-----------------------

  1. Open a terminal in the project folder
  2. Type:   npm run dev
  3. Open:   http://localhost:3000
  4. Edit the file and SAVE - the site updates by itself, no restart needed

When you are happy:

  git add -A
  git commit -m "Updated my details"
  git push

Vercel rebuilds automatically. Your live site updates in ~2 minutes.


THE GOLDEN RULE
---------------

  ANYTHING YOU LEAVE EMPTY DISAPPEARS.

  - One project? You see one project. Not one plus three empty boxes.
  - No jobs yet? The Career Timeline building is never built, and the city
    fills that space with ordinary buildings instead.
  - Add your first job next year and the building appears on its own.

You cannot "break the layout" by having too little. It adjusts itself.

  PART 1 - YOUR INFORMATION
===============================================================================

Everything below is inside src/config/portfolio.ts. Just change the text
between the quote marks. Keep the quote marks and the commas.


--- YOUR NAME AND TITLE -------------------------------------------------------

Find:  identity: {

    name: 'YOUR NAME',                    <- your real name
    callsign: 'CYBER_ARCHITECT',          <- your gamer-tag, shown on the HUD
    title: 'Full-Stack & 3D Web Developer',
    tagline: 'I build immersive interfaces...',   <- one line under your name
    location: 'Your City, Country',
    availability: 'Open to opportunities',
    avatarInitials: 'YN',                 <- 2 letters, shown in a box

WARNING: keep the straight quotes '  like this. If your text contains an
apostrophe (for example  it's ) write it as  it\'s  or use double quotes "".


--- ABOUT YOU -----------------------------------------------------------------

Find:  about: {

    paragraphs: [
      'First paragraph about you.',
      'Second paragraph.',
    ],

Add or remove paragraphs freely. Each one needs quotes and a comma.
Empty the list entirely and the whole "Operator Profile" building disappears.

    facts: [
      { label: 'Focus', value: '3D Web / AI Interfaces' },
      { label: 'Experience', value: 'X+ years' },
    ],

Little info boxes. Add or delete lines as you like.

    logLines: [
      'Started coding at age __.',
    ],

Fun facts that type out one by one like a terminal. Optional - empty it to
remove that section.


--- EMAIL AND RESUME ----------------------------------------------------------

Find:  links: {

    email: 'you@example.com',
    resumeUrl: '/resume.pdf',

To add your CV: put your PDF in the  public  folder and name it exactly
resume.pdf. That is all - the download button starts working.

To remove the download button: set   resumeUrl: ''


--- SOCIAL LINKS (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, X...) ---------------------------

Find:  socials: [

    socials: [
      { platform: 'github', url: 'https://github.com/yourhandle' },
      { platform: 'linkedin', url: 'https://linkedin.com/in/yourhandle' },

      { platform: 'instagram', url: 'https://instagram.com/yourhandle' },
      { platform: 'x', url: 'https://x.com/yourhandle' },

REMOVE A LINK: delete its line. It vanishes everywhere.

REORDER: the order here is the order on screen.

Icons already built in:

    behance     artstation  itch        website     email

You can add a label so it reads nicely:

COLOURS: assigned automatically. You do not need to pick one.

--- YOUR OWN IMAGES (on the buildings, and on the spaceship) -------------------

    These do NOT go in portfolio.ts. They have their own folder:

        src/assets/images/

    Drop files in, save, done. No paths to type, no code to edit.

        building-01.jpg  ... up to ...  building-10.jpg
              Each becomes a glowing panel that slowly circles one of the
              towers. They are spread along the route you walk, and met in
              number order, so building-01 is the first one you pass.

        orbiter.jpg
              The banner carried by the ship that circles outside the map.

    .jpg .jpeg .png and .webp all work, and you can mix them. Add one file or
    ten - whatever is in the folder is what shows up, and empty slots simply
    do not exist. Use the two-digit numbers (01, not 1) or building-10 will
    sort before building-2.

    Keep them around 1024 pixels or smaller. Graphics memory is used up by an
    image's DIMENSIONS, not its file size, so a huge photo is what makes
    phones stutter even when the file looks small.

    There is a fuller note in  src/assets/images/README.txt .

    To change how they BEHAVE - building colours, whether the panels circle
    the towers or stay put, and the ship's size, speed, height and shape -
    open  src/config/media.ts . Every setting in there is commented.

    To turn the ship off entirely, set  enabled: false  in that file. To make
    it a drifting rock instead, set  kind: 'asteroid' .


--- THE SETTING (space station or neon city) ----------------------------------

    Find  world:  in portfolio.ts and change the one word:

        world: {
          setting: 'space-station',      <- an orbital hangar deck, stars above
        },

    The two choices are:

        'space-station'   plated deck, station modules, drifting dust, open
                          space and a distant sun overhead
        'neon-city'       rain-soaked street at night, neon towers, wet grid

    This changes the SCENERY ONLY. Your content, the landmarks, the layout,
    the map, the navigation arrows, the character and every objective are
    identical either way - so switch back and forth as often as you like and
    nothing is lost.

    The neon theme colours (ESC -> SETTINGS) still apply to both.


--- THE OPENING CINEMATIC -----------------------------------------------------

Find:  intro: {

    enabled: true,
    durationSeconds: 3,

    enabled: false        -> skip it entirely, spawn straight into the city
    durationSeconds: 5    -> make it longer
    durationSeconds: 2    -> make it shorter

Visitors can always skip it with a click or any key.


===============================================================================
  PART 2 - CHANGING THE CHARACTER MODEL
===============================================================================

Right now the character is "PrototypePete" from KayKit. It is CC0 (public
domain) so it is completely safe to use commercially, no credit required.

The settings live in the same file:

    character: {
      modelUrl: '/models/character.glb',
      animationUrls: [],
      height: 1.85,
      yawOffset: 0,
      clips: { idle: '', walk: '', run: '', jump: '', fall: '' },
    },

The game accepts  .glb  .gltf  and  .fbx  files. No conversion needed.


--- OPTION A: a model that ALREADY has animations -----------------------------

  1. Put the file in the  public/models  folder
  2. Change one line:

        modelUrl: '/models/my-character.glb',

  3. Reload. Done.


--- OPTION B: a model with NO animations (use Mixamo) -------------------------

Mixamo is a free Adobe site that adds a skeleton and animations to a plain
model. Go to  https://www.mixamo.com  and sign in with a free Adobe account.

  1. Click UPLOAD CHARACTER and pick your model
     (accepts .fbx .obj or a .zip - NOT .glb)

     IMPORTANT: if your model came with texture images, put the model file and
     all the texture files together in ONE folder with no sub-folders, then zip
     that. Mixamo cannot find textures hidden inside a "textures" sub-folder.

  2. Place the markers it asks for (chin, wrists, elbows, knees). ~1 minute.

  3. Now download FOUR animations. Search for them by name:

     ANIMATION      DOWNLOAD SETTINGS
     -----------    --------------------------------------------------
     Idle           Format: FBX Binary   Skin: WITH SKIN
     Walking        Format: FBX Binary   Skin: Without Skin   + IN PLACE
     Running        Format: FBX Binary   Skin: Without Skin   + IN PLACE
     Jump           Format: FBX Binary   Skin: Without Skin

     For all four:  Frames per second = 30 ,  Keyframe reduction = none

     TICK THE "IN PLACE" CHECKBOX ON WALKING AND RUNNING if you see it.
     If you forget, it is handled anyway - the game strips the built-in
     forward travel out of every animation automatically. Without that, a
     walk cycle carries the character forward and then snaps it back on every
     loop, which looks like walking and being yanked backwards over and over.

     The "Idle / With Skin" download is special - that file IS your character
     (body + skeleton). The other three are animation data only.

     Optional extra: "Falling Idle" (Without Skin) makes long falls look better.

  4. Put all four files in  public/models

  5. Update the config:

        character: {
          modelUrl: '/models/Idle.fbx',
          animationUrls: [
            '/models/Walking.fbx',
            '/models/Running.fbx',
            '/models/Jump.fbx',
          ],
          height: 1.85,
          yawOffset: 0,
          clips: { idle: '', walk: '', run: '', jump: '', fall: '' },
        },

  6. Reload and check it walks properly.


--- IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG --------------------------------------------------

Press F12 in your browser and click the Console tab. The game tells you
exactly what it found:

    [portfolio] character loaded with 4 clips: [mixamo.com, Walking, ...]
    [portfolio] mapped to motion states: {idle: Idle, walk: Walking, ...}
    [portfolio] model bounds 1.18 x 1.90 x 1.12, scaled by 0.974 to reach 1.85m

  Character is lying down or facing backwards?
      yawOffset: 180

  Character is too big or too small?
      It scales automatically. If it still looks off, change  height: 1.85

  A movement is not playing?
      Look at the "mapped to motion states" line. If something says  null ,
      copy the exact clip name from the list above it into clips:

          clips: { idle: 'Idle_A', walk: 'Walking', run: 'Run_Fwd', jump: '', fall: '' },

  Nothing loads at all?
      The game falls back to a simple blocky character. Check the filename in
      modelUrl matches the real file EXACTLY, including capital letters.

===============================================================================
  PART 3 - COMMON QUESTIONS
===============================================================================

HOW DO I CHANGE THE COLOURS?
    In the game, press ESC -> SETTINGS -> NEON THEME. Four themes.
    To change the default, find  theme: 'cyan'  in src/store/useGameStore.ts
    and use 'magenta', 'emerald' or 'amber'.

THE GAME RUNS SLOWLY ON MY MACHINE
    It now measures its own framerate and lowers the graphics automatically
    if it drops below about 40fps, telling you when it does.

    To set it yourself: ESC -> SETTINGS -> GRAPHICS. Choosing a level by hand
    switches the automatic adjustment off, so your choice sticks.

    LOW turns off bloom and particles entirely and is the fastest.

HOW DO I CHANGE THE BROWSER TAB TITLE?
    Open  index.html  and edit the <title> line near the top.

THE NAMES ON THE MAP ARE TOO LONG / I WANT DIFFERENT ONES
    The big map uses the landmark's full name. The little corner radar uses a
    short version, because a long one does not fit.

    Both live in src/systems/world.ts . Find the landmark you want and edit:

        name:  'AI RESEARCH LAB',      <- shown on the big map and in the city
        short: 'AI LAB',               <- shown on the corner radar

    Keep "short" to about eight characters or it will be skipped to avoid
    overlapping its neighbours.

HOW DO I CHANGE THE HIDDEN COLLECTIBLES?
    src/systems/world.ts , find COLLECTIBLES. The numbers are positions:
    [left/right, height, forward/back].

I BROKE IT AND THE PAGE IS BLANK
    Almost always a missing comma or quote mark in portfolio.ts.
    Press F12, read the red error - it names the line number.
    Or undo your change:   git checkout src/config/portfolio.ts

HOW DO I PUT IT ONLINE?
    Already done - it is on Vercel. Every  git push  updates the live site
    automatically in about two minutes.


===============================================================================
  THE CONTROLS (for your own reference)
===============================================================================

    Click the view ...... captures the mouse so it steers the camera
    (just walking) ...... pressing W A S D also captures it, so the mouse
                          steers from the first step without clicking first
    ALT ................. releases the mouse so you can click buttons.
                          Walking will NOT take it back until you click.
    W A S D or arrows ... walk
    Mouse ............... look around (once captured)
    Shift ............... sprint - a SPRINTING badge appears when it is on
    Space ............... jump
    E ................... open the thing you are standing at
    M ................... open / close the full city map
    Esc ................. pause menu (Resume / Settings / Restart / Main Menu)
    ` (backtick) ........ developer terminal, type "help"

The objective panel in the bottom-left shows itself when the objective
changes, then folds into a small tab. Click the tab to open it again.

On phones a joystick, jump and sprint buttons appear instead. Drag the right
side of the screen to look around. Tap the small radar on the right-hand side
to open the full map.


THE MAP AND FINDING YOUR WAY
----------------------------

    Press M (or tap the radar on a phone) to open the city map. It lists every
    landmark and every data core you have not picked up yet, with how far away
    each one is.

    Pick one and press NAVIGATE. The map closes and a line of glowing arrows
    appears on the street showing the way. The arrows route AROUND the
    buildings - they never point through one - and they redraw themselves if
    you wander off or take a different street.

    The badge at the top of the screen shows where you are heading and how far
    is left. Press the small x on it, or STOP on the map, to cancel.
    The arrows disappear on their own when you arrive.

    Nothing here needs configuring. Landmarks come from your own content in
    portfolio.ts, so the map always lists exactly what your city contains.
